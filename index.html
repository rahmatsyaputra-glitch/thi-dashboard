Timmy Happiness Index survey is IDN's self administered Engagement Survey for all Timmy in IDN. The survey assess 11 areas with 44 questions/items.

In 2025, some changes have been made to the Creative Lab area, including renaming it to Workplace Support and refining the questions for better clarity. This adjustment aims to better assess how IDN provides the necessary support to enhance both productivity and well-being for Timmys in the workplace.

Comparative Analysis
1. Perception (Personal Believe)

This analysis compares how Timmy perceive their own contribution versus how they perceive the company's support. The goal is to identify whether we have alignment or gaps between Timmy effort and organizational enablement.

3 Key Gaps in our organization are:

A. Ownership & Recognition
Ownership → "Employees take Responsibility"
Recognition → "Company acknowledges contributions"
B. Growth Mindset and Development Support
Growth mindset → "Employees are willing to learn and improve"
Development support → "Company provides learning opportunities"
C. Work Ownership & Workplace Support
Work ownership → "Employees take responsibility for delivering results"
Workplace support → "Tools, environment, facilities enable them"
2. Leadership

Leadership is a key stakeholder in shaping and sustaining team culture. As the primary drivers of team performance and employee experience, leaders play a critical role as agents of culture within the organization.

This section highlights the alignment between managerial and non-managerial Timmy across key execution areas. The results should be considered as an important reflection of how effectively leadership is translating expectations into day-to-day team experiences.

We evaluate three critical areas of execution:
1. How to achieve OKR (clarity & guidance)
2. Work ownership (accountability culture)
3. Communication (information flow & support)
